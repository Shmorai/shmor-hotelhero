Booking a hotel with kids

  • Enter every guest. Prices and room options depend on how many adults and children are staying, so add children and their ages on the booking site.
  • Check the beds. “Family room” can mean very different set-ups. Look for the bed types and whether cots are available.
  • Two rooms can beat one. Sometimes two connecting or standard rooms cost about the same as one large family room.
  • School holidays cost more. Book early, or compare dates just outside the holidays.

Frequently asked questions

Do kids stay free in UK hotels?
Some hotels and chains let young children stay free when sharing with adults, but rules differ. Always add children to your search on the booking site to see the right price.
