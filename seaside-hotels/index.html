Booking a seaside hotel

  • Sea view or not? Check the room type: sea-view rooms usually cost more.
  • Summer books up early. School holidays and bank holidays are the busiest times on the coast.
  • Try shoulder season. Spring and autumn can bring lower prices and quieter beaches.
  • Parking. Small harbour towns often have limited parking, so check what the hotel offers.

When is the cheapest time for a UK seaside break?
