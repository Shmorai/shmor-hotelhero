Tips for booking a hotel at the last minute

  • Compare quickly, but compare. Last-minute prices can vary a lot between sites.
  • Check check-in times. If you’re arriving late, make sure the hotel has a 24-hour reception or a late check-in option.
  • Use the apps’ maps. Most booking sites let you search around your current location.
  • Read the cancellation terms. Same-day bookings are often non-refundable.

Frequently asked questions

Are last-minute hotel deals really cheaper?
Sometimes. Hotels with empty rooms may lower prices close to the date, but in busy periods prices can also go up. Comparing several sites is the best way to see what’s available right now.
