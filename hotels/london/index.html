Finding a good-value London hotel

  • Zone 1 vs further out. Areas like Stratford, Canary Wharf or Hammersmith can offer more space for the money, with fast tube links into the centre.
  • Weekends can be cheaper. Hotels that rely on business travellers, such as around the City and Canary Wharf, often drop prices on Friday and Saturday nights.
  • Station hotels save time. Arriving by train? King’s Cross, Euston, Paddington and Waterloo all have plenty of hotels nearby.
  • Driving? Central London has the Congestion Charge and the ULEZ, and parking is expensive. Check before you book a hotel with parking.

Airport hotels in London

Early flight? Hotels at or near Heathrow, Gatwick, Stansted, Luton and London City let you skip a dawn journey across town. See last-minute hotels if you need a room tonight.

Frequently asked questions

What’s the best area to stay in London for first-timers?
The West End (Covent Garden, Soho, Leicester Square) and the South Bank put you within walking distance of many sights and theatres. They’re popular, so compare prices across sites and consider areas a few tube stops away for better value.
Are London hotels cheaper at weekends?
Often, especially in business districts like the City and Canary Wharf. Around the West End, weekends and school holidays can be busier, so it’s worth comparing a few different dates.
