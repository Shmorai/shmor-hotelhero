Manchester hotels on event days

Big concerts, football matches and conferences can fill Manchester hotels and push prices up. If your trip lines up with a big event, compare early, and consider staying a little further out along the Metrolink tram network.

Frequently asked questions

Which area of Manchester is best for a weekend?
The Northern Quarter is known for independent bars and shops, Deansgate and Spinningfields for restaurants and smarter hotels, and Salford Quays for MediaCityUK and waterside views, linked by tram.
