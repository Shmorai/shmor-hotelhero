Choosing where to stay in a UK city

  • Central is handy, but not always best value. In big cities, hotels a few stops out on the tube, tram or train can cost less and still be quick to reach.
  • Check for events. Concerts, football, festivals and graduation weeks fill hotels fast and push prices up.
  • Parking matters. Many city-centre hotels charge for parking or have none, so check before you drive.
