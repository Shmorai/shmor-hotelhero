Sea view or side street?

Sea-view rooms on the seafront usually cost more than rooms at the back or in the streets behind. Check the exact room type when you compare, so you know whether you’re looking at a sea view. Summer weekends and bank holidays are the busiest times.

Frequently asked questions

Is Brighton a good day trip or weekend break from London?
Both. Direct trains from London take around an hour, so it’s easy as a day trip, but staying lets you enjoy the seafront in the evening. See also our seaside hotels page.
