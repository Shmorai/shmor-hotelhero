Before you book with your dog

  • Check the pet fee. Many hotels charge a per-night or per-stay fee for dogs.
  • Check the rules. Hotels may limit the number or size of dogs, or which rooms and areas they’re allowed in.
  • Tell the hotel. Add your dog to the booking or message the hotel so a suitable room is ready.
  • Beach rules. Some beaches restrict dogs in summer, so check local signs.
