Making the most of a weekend break

  • City hotels can be cheaper at weekends, when business travellers go home.
  • Countryside and seaside hotels often cost more at weekends, so try Sunday–Tuesday for a bargain.
  • Ask about late checkout. It makes a Sunday far more relaxed, and some sites include it on member rates.

Frequently asked questions

How far ahead should I book a weekend break?
For popular cities and busy dates, earlier is usually better. For quieter weekends, last-minute deals can appear. Comparing across sites shows you the best price for your dates either way.
